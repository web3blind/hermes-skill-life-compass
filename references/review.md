# Weekly review grounded in actual history

Weekly review is optional, not a requirement to maintain a Compass. If enabled, choose a local day/time and a **specific authorized conversation/source** with the user; protect days off if they prefer. Do not silently combine unrelated chats. Use the exact previous seven days in the user's time zone. The assistant retrieves that agreed source's accessible history and optional feedback itself: do not make the person reconstruct an accessible week by hand. Verify source identity and time-window coverage; if the agreed source cannot be read, do not fall back to another chat as though it were equivalent.

## Evidence discipline

- Separate the user's own words and actions from agent messages, scheduled reminders, copied quotations, forwarded posts and someone else's work. An agent finishing late does not prove that the user worked late.
- Keep date/message anchors internally for each observation. If tools return only a partial window, report the coverage limitation; no messages does **not** prove a quiet life.
- Mention 1–3 actual signals when present: a useful decision, progress, a repaired error, a changed priority, overload, a deliberate refusal, rest or a process worth simplifying. Recognize factual wins where supported, without generic flattery.
- A missed check-in, one late night, a 5, or silence about a life direction is not evidence of personal failure. Repeated patterns require independent observations. Do not fabricate health claims, metrics, causal links or remembered activity.
- Feedback helps tune timing, frequency and effort, not grade the week. Do not sum 0–5 values into an average achievement score or equate 0 with failure.

Keep the review short: a few concrete facts, one calm inference, and **at most one** question if there is a useful decision to make. If there is no meaningful signal, say that and stop. A quiet or low-output week needs no compensatory action. Weekly is a good time to check whether reminders are still useful; do not change them without consent. Do not auto-create a monthly reset or another scheduled audit.

## When coverage is incomplete

```text
I could only see part of the week, so this is not a full review. The one supported observation is <fact and time>; I cannot infer what happened elsewhere.
```

When exact history retrieval is unavailable, rely only on user-provided notes with a clearly marked limit or skip the review. Never invent a diary from a cron schedule.
