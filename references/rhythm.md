# Directions, reminders, and optional feedback

## Set up a rhythm only on request

Ask the user for their own direction names (five is the default template; fewer/more are fine), why each matters, which days and local times are welcome, the time zone, preferred destination, and which reminders should be disabled. One direction can have several small rhythms; some directions need no reminder at all. Avoid copying someone else's schedule or turning every area into a daily job. A specific appointment with an exact time belongs in a calendar, not a direction.

Each enabled reminder should contain only:
1. one calm sentence linking to that direction;
2. a **small optional step** or an honest pause, within the person's stated energy/time;
3. an explicit enough-for-now boundary;
4. optional 0–5 feedback (not required every time if it creates noise).

Example — generic, **not** an active scheduled job:

```text
A gentle check-in for the direction you chose.
One possible step: give it ten minutes, or leave it for another day if it is not needed now.
Enough for today: no catch-up debt.
If you want, reply with 0–5 to describe relevance and effort. This is calibration, not a report.
```

## Fixed 0–5 semantics

- 0: not needed, or done beforehand. No replacement work.
- 1: done with pleasure.
- 2: done, slightly strained or tired.
- 3: done, tired.
- 4: did it, but it was irritating/exasperating; not a synonym for tiredness.
- 5: did not do it today; why remains unknown. Not a judgment.

Map an unambiguous reply to the **particular reminder** only when the platform exposes message/thread context; otherwise ask which direction the reply refers to. Do not attribute numbers quoted from other people or forwarded messages. Missing replies are unknown. Do not count an agent's own activity as the user's completion. Keep an optional minimal record only with consent, e.g. date, direction ID, reminder ID and rating. Do not harvest whole chats into a public skill repository.

After a handful of voluntary replies or in a requested weekly review, look for *patterns*, not grades: many 1s may indicate a pleasant rhythm; repeated 3s suggest less load; repeated 4s call for pausing, shortening or moving the activity; repeated 5s call for checking relevance, timing and energy without shame. A 0 is healthy irrelevance or prior completion. Ask the person before changing cadence. Do not generate substitute work, an execution percentage, or a 'streak'.

## Other useful contours

- **Bedtime transition:** optional one reminder near the chosen time; save a one-line continuation if an idea is active, or place a bookmark in a book, then reduce new incoming information. A person may choose their own realistic belief and wording to be repeated naturally here; never invent, strengthen, or repeat it in unrelated tasks. Optional pre-existing audio can be offered only through a verified media route with consent. Do not demand a sleep report.
- **Relationships and rest:** do not assume that silence in chat means neglect. Ask about after-effect, not the medium: a chosen book or message may be restorative; an endless feed may not. Weekend activity can be voluntary and restorative.
- **Timed commitments:** if asked, calendar events can have separate nearby reminders. Check access, timezone, permissions, duplicates and notification target. Do not automatically move direction reminders into the calendar or require a special vendor.
- **Money-related actions:** a reminder never authorizes a payment, account change or ad spend. Stop and request approval at the transaction boundary.
